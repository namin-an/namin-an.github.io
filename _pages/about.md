Creative. Dilligent. Motivated. Open-Minded. Resourceful.

These are the attributes that I strive to embody as an AI researcher. I believe that AI can meaningfully assist humans in complex tasks, such as simulating visual prosthetic devices for people with visually impairments. This vision shaped my first research experience at <a href='https://imvisionlab.com/'>Im Lab</a> as an research intern under the guidance of <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jidhBw0AAAAJ&hl=en'>Maesoon Im</a>.

Since March 2023, I have been a full-time graduate student at <a href='https://xfact.net/'>XFact Lab</a>, co-adviced by Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hao9RrgAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>James Thorne</a> and Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KB5XZGIAAAAJ&hl=en'>Hyunjung Shim</a>. From March to May 2025, I was a visiting researcher at <a href='https://www.copenlu.com/'>CopeNLU</a>, collaborating with Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=DjJp0dcAAAAJ&hl'>Isabelle Augenstein</a> and Dr. <a href='https://yoonnajang.github.io/'>Yoonna Jang</a>.

My current research interest focuses on developing assistive, trustworthy AI and ML systems, with particular emphasis on mitigating modality and social biases in multimodal and generative models. Please feel free to contact me at <a href="mailto:naminan@kaist.ac.kr">naminan@kaist.ac.kr</a>!

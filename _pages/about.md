Creative. Dilligent. Motivated. Open-Minded. Resourceful.

These are the qualities that I strive to embody as an AI researcher. I believe that AI can meaningfully assist people, such as simulating visual prostheses for individuals with visually impairments. This vision shaped my first research experience as a research intern at <a href='https://imvisionlab.com/'>Im Lab</a> under the guidance of Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jidhBw0AAAAJ&hl=en'>Maesoon Im</a>.

I am currently a graduate student at <a href='https://kaist-cvml.github.io/'>CVML Lab</a>, adviced by Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KB5XZGIAAAAJ&hl=en'>Hyunjung Shim</a> (Dr. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hao9RrgAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>James Thorne</a> from 2023-2025). I am excited to start an internship at  <a href='https://www.naverlabs.com/en/'>NAVER AI Lab</a> starting April 2026, guided by Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jcP7m1QAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao'>Dongyoon Han</a>.

My current research interest focuses on developing assistive, trustworthy AI and ML systems, with particular emphasis on mitigating modality and social biases in multimodal and generative models from interpretability perspectives.
